Locked-off tripod shot: the camera never moves, pans or zooms. Empty nightclub, no people. The room stays exactly as in the first frame: the dark concrete walls, the two speaker boxes, the stone DJ booth and the glossy concrete floor do not move or change shape.

Only the two LED screens change: the long LED ceiling and the LED wall above the DJ booth. Together they are one continuous screen folded at the far end, and they always show one single image drawn in the room's perspective, perfectly aligned with the edges of the screens, like a window into a deep glowing space beyond the ceiling and the wall. The screen edges stay sharp and straight; nothing spills outside the screens except their light.

On the screens: a slow, glossy wave of liquid silver light rises out of the bottom of the far LED wall, climbs the wall, folds over onto the ceiling and rolls along the whole ceiling toward the camera, followed by a second, softer wave. Behind the waves the color drifts smoothly from deep red to warm orange and soft magenta, then back to red. Soft, smooth gradients, low contrast, no hard patterns, no text, no logos, no symbols.

The changing colors of the screens tint the walls, the booth and the reflections on the floor.

Sound: a soft, low ambient pulse, no voices.
