One continuous shot with no cuts: a slow, steady forward flight through the CLUBE universe, the surreal world shown in @Image 1.

The world: a vast plain of pale sand and rough beige stone under a warm gradient sky, deep dark red at the top of the frame, through red and orange, to soft magenta and pink haze at the horizon, with a few tiny stars. Colossal statues stand like monuments on both sides of the path and far into the distance, made of silver pixels and small voxel blocks with a brushed chrome sheen: the striding woman with sunglasses, streaming hair and a raised cocktail glass of @Image 2, the DJ holding up a vinyl record of @Image 3, and the dancer of @Image 4. Huge slabs of rough beige stone float in the air above the camera. Clusters of glowing violet and magenta crystals made of tiny pixels grow from the ground and from the floating stones. Fine silver particles and soft haze drift in the air.

Camera: wide-angle lens, low height, perfectly level, always looking straight ahead along the path, with a slight upward angle so the sky and the tops of the statues fill most of the frame and the horizon sits low in the frame. The camera only moves forward, at one constant slow speed, without turning, panning, tilting, rolling or shaking. As we advance, the statues, floating stones and crystals grow larger and slide outward past the edges of the frame and above the camera with strong parallax, and new ones keep appearing ahead, so the upper part of the frame is always full of detail.

Photorealistic, cinematic, rich detail, sharp focus. No people, no text, no logos, no room, no walls, no screens, no frames.

Sound: an airy shimmering pad with a soft deep whoosh.
