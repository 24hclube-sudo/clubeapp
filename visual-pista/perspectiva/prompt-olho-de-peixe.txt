One continuous shot with no cuts, filmed with a 180-degree fisheye lens pointing straight up at the sky while the camera glides slowly forward at walking height through the CLUBE universe of @Image 1.

Lens and framing: true equidistant fisheye. The center of the frame is the zenith, straight overhead. The direction of travel is the right edge of the frame: the horizon ahead appears as a curved line hugging the right edge, and the horizon behind as a curved line hugging the left edge, with only a thin strip of pale sand ground beyond each horizon. The long side of the frame runs from the horizon ahead to the horizon behind, through the sky overhead. The camera never rotates, tilts, pans or rolls; it only moves forward at one constant slow speed.

The world: a warm gradient sky, deep dark red overhead, through red and orange, to soft magenta and pink haze near both horizons, with a few tiny stars. Colossal statues of silver pixels and small voxel blocks with a brushed chrome sheen tower over the path, seen from below: the striding woman with sunglasses, streaming hair and a raised cocktail glass of @Image 2, the DJ holding up a vinyl record of @Image 3, and the dancer of @Image 4. Huge slabs of rough beige stone float overhead at different heights, with clusters of glowing violet and magenta pixel crystals on them. Fine silver particles drift in the air.

Motion: new statues, stones and crystals rise from the horizon at the right edge, grow larger as we approach, pass overhead through the center of the frame with strong parallax (closer things move faster), and sink toward the horizon at the left edge. The frame is always full of detail.

Photorealistic, cinematic, sharp focus. No people, no text, no logos, no buildings, no straight architectural lines, no frames, no vignette.

Sound: an airy shimmering pad with a soft deep whoosh.
